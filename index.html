About Morpheus Media Relations

Our Ethos

Authentic storytelling, trusted relationships, and thoughtful execution.

                    Morpheus Media Relations is a boutique agency dedicated to the strategic elevation of cultural icons and prestige projects. We believe that true influence is not bought, but cultivated through narrative precision and unwavering integrity.                

                    In an era of fleeting attention, we specialize in longevity. Our approach combines the rigor of traditional publicity with the agility of modern digital strategy, ensuring our clients don't just join the conversation—they define it.                

Leadership

Ayrton Dimitri

Founder & Creative Director

"The most powerful currency in media isn't noise; it's trust."

Before founding Morpheus Media Relations, Ayrton was navigating the upper echelons of talent management and international entertainment. His unique perspective blends business acumen with a deep respect for the creative process.

Under his direction, the agency has grown from a specialized PR firm into a full-service media consultancy known for its "quiet luxury" approach—achieving massive results through surgical precision rather than sheer volume.
